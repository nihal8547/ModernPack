Our Beginning

From a Small Farm to a Trusted Brand

Founded in 2010 by the Nair family in Ernakulam, ModernPack began as a small rice processing unit with a single mission: deliver honest, pure food products that families can trust.

Over the years, we have grown into one of Kerala's leading food product companies with state-of-the-art processing facilities, a diverse product portfolio of 500+ items, and a distribution network spanning all of India.

Our roots in Kerala's agricultural heritage drive everything we do — from sourcing directly from farmers to maintaining zero-compromise quality standards in every pack that leaves our facility.
