FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity? ▾

Minimum order quantity varies by product. For most products it is 50kg. For bulk institutional orders, we offer flexible terms. Contact us for product-specific MOQ.

Do you provide samples before bulk orders? ▾

Yes! We provide free product samples (up to 500g per product, up to 5 products) to verified businesses. Submit a sample request through our contact form.

What certifications do your products carry? ▾

All products are FSSAI and ISO 22000:2018 certified. Select products also carry NPOP Organic, APEDA, and GI Tag certifications. Lab test reports are available on request.

Do you offer custom private label packaging? ▾

Yes, we offer private labelling and custom packaging for bulk orders above 500kg. Minimum lead time is 15 days. Contact our sales team for a detailed quote.

What are your delivery timelines? ▾

Within Kerala: 1-2 business days. Pan-India: 3-7 business days depending on location. Express delivery options are available at additional cost.

Can I become a ModernPack distributor? ▾

We are actively expanding our distributor network across India. If you are interested in becoming a distributor in your region, please fill out our contact form with "Distributor Partnership" as the inquiry type.

Do you export to other countries? ▾

Yes, we export to UAE, Kuwait, Bahrain, Singapore, Malaysia, and the UK. We are APEDA registered and comply with international food safety standards.

What payment modes do you accept? ▾

We accept bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS), UPI, cheques, and credit terms (30 days) for established accounts. International payments via wire transfer/LC.
